SoonSure
Plans are Soon. Records are Sure.

Past and future
on one sheet of paper

A day is a clock face: a two-lap spiral from midnight to midnight, your plans along it, a red pin for now. A week is one row, months are one page. Pinch like a map — zoom in for a day, out for a season.

A plan is Soon: not settled yet, so it's a dotted arc cut on a ten-minute grid.
A record is Sure: it happened, so it's one solid block.

Time passing never turns Soon into Sure. Tap a plan whose time has come and it opens as a diary form, already filled in. You turn it into a record by tapping Confirm — as is, or with a line about how it went.

The same paper is a ledger. Switch to Money and payments appear as thin ticks on the lane, each day's spending as a ring colored by category, each week's net in the margin. A budget is a Soon amount; a payment is a Sure one. On Apple Watch, four complications put it on your wrist.

Your data stays on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.
